1. SCOPE AND OBJECTIVE
This standard operating procedure defines the detection thresholds, automated mitigation,
and triage escalation for UPI bank switch degradations across partner banks (HDFC, ICICI,
SBI, AXIS, KOTAK) and payment gateways (Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU).

2. SEVERITY THRESHOLDS
- CRITICAL: Success rate (SR) drop >= 30 percentage points below rolling 24-hour baseline,
  or switch latency exceeds 5,000ms for 3 consecutive 5-minute sampling windows, or total
  blocked transaction volume exceeds INR 500,000.
- HIGH: SR drop between 20 and 29 percentage points, or switch latency between 3,000ms and
  4,999ms, or repeated BAD_REQUEST_GATEWAY_DOWN errors affecting >= 3 enterprise merchants.
- MEDIUM: SR drop between 10 and 19 percentage points, or switch latency between 2,000ms
  and 2,999ms.
- LOW / INFO: Minor jitter with SR drop < 10 percentage points and latency < 2,000ms.

3. IMMEDIATE OPERATIONAL MITIGATION (DYNAMIC ROUTING)
When a bank switch shows sustained failure rates exceeding 25% or gateway timeout errors:
- Step 1: Temporarily downgrade traffic allocation to the degraded banking switch by 80%.
- Step 2: Route eligible UPI Intent and Collect requests through alternate bank handles
  or secondary gateway rails.
- Step 3: Trigger active health probes every 60 seconds with synthetic micro-transactions.
- Step 4: Re-route traffic incrementally (20% -> 50% -> 100%) only after 15 consecutive
  minutes of stable success rate >= 92%.

4. MERCHANT NOTIFICATION & DOWNTIME BANNER
- For HIGH and CRITICAL switch degradations lasting > 5 minutes, post real-time status page
  advisories to affected merchants via the dashboard and webhook push notifications.
- Inform merchant support teams to suppress user checkout retries for the degraded bank.
