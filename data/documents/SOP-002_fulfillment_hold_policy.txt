TITLE: Fulfillment Hold Policy for Pending Bank Debits
DOCUMENT_ID: SOP-002
DOCUMENT_TYPE: POLICY
VERSION: 1.0
EFFECTIVE_DATE: 2026-02-01
DEPARTMENT: Payment Operations
ACCESS_LEVEL: INTERNAL
CLASSIFICATION: SYNTHETIC

1. PURPOSE AND APPLICABILITY
This policy governs merchant order fulfillment and inventory release during asynchronous
payment states, specifically when customers report money debited from bank accounts
while gateway status indicates PENDING or AUTHORIZED.

2. SUSPEND FULFILLMENT ON PENDING DEBITS
- If a transaction is in BANK_DEBITED_PAYMENT_PENDING state, the merchant MUST place
  goods dispatch or digital entitlement on a temporary 30-minute hold.
- Under no circumstances should the merchant auto-cancel the order or ask the customer
  to retry the payment immediately, as this leads to duplicate customer debits.

3. WEBHOOK AND POLLING SYNCHRONIZATION
- Payment gateway status reconciliation operates asynchronously. The webhook gateway
  attempts real-time delivery with exponential backoff retries over a 4-hour window.
- The platform queries NPCI / Bank Switch status every 5 minutes for pending transactions.
- Once the webhook receives payment.captured, release fulfillment automatically.
- If final status is payment.failed or terminal after 24 hours, trigger auto-refund or
  release inventory with clear merchant dashboard notification.
