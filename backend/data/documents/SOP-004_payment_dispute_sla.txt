1. PURPOSE
Defines time-bound actions required when card networks or acquiring banks initiate
dispute notifications (retrieval requests, chargebacks, pre-arbitration).

2. DISPUTE PHASES AND MERCHANT SLAS
- INQUIRY: Merchant has 7 calendar days to upload proof of delivery or invoice.
- CHARGEBACK: Formal dispute by cardholder. Merchant must provide proof within 5 days.
- PRE-ARBITRATION: Second-level review requiring compliance officer authorization.

3. AUTO-RESERVATION AND REFUND RULES
- If a dispute is raised with reason FRAUDULENT and merchant dispute rate exceeds 1.0%
  of GMV, temporarily reserve 100% of disputed amount from the next settlement cycle.
- If merchant fails to respond within SLA, accept liability and debit reserve.
