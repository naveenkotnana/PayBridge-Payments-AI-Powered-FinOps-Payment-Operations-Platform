1. OVERVIEW
This procedure defines operational workflows for detecting, triaging, and resolving
discrepancies between captured transactions, merchant settlements, and bank MIS files.

2. DISCREPANCY TYPES AND THRESHOLDS
- MDR_MISMATCH: Merchant Discount Rate fee charged exceeds contracted pricing schedule
  by >= INR 1.00. Action: Auto-flag for finance operations review; generate credit adjustment.
- UNCAPTURED_AUTH: Payment authorized at bank switch but not captured within 5 business days.
  Action: Auto-expire authorization and release customer bank hold.
- MISSING_GATEWAY_SETTLEMENT: Transaction marked CAPTURED on gateway but absent in nodal
  settlement batch file. Action: Quarantine settlement run and query gateway settlement API.
- DUPLICATE_DEBIT: Customer charged twice for single order_id. Action: Instant automated
  refund initiated on secondary payment_id with speed=INSTANT.

3. ESCALATION MATRIX
- Variances > INR 50,000 or delayed settlements > T+2 days require tier-2 escalation to
  Finance Controller and Merchant Account Lead within 2 hours.
- Generate automated reconciliation adjustment entry in PostgreSQL ledger with audit trail.
